Ph.D. Student in Convergence IT Engineering (CiTE) · POSTECH

Hello! I'm Jongsoo, a Ph.D. student at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Soohee Han.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning under delayed feedback in real-world robotic systems, aiming to help agents make better decisions in the presence of sensing, actuation, and communication delays.

I'm always happy to connect, exchange ideas, and explore new research collaborations. If you have any questions or just want to chat about research, feel free to reach out!
